## What we collect

When you place an order we ask for:

- your name and phone number (we use your number for WhatsApp and SMS updates)
- your email address, if you choose to give it
- your delivery area and address, and a GhanaPost GPS address if you give one
- what you ordered and what you have paid

## What we do not collect

We do **not** see or store your card number or your Mobile Money PIN. Payments are handled by our payment provider. We only keep a reference and the amount.

## How we use it

- to process and deliver your order
- to send order updates and balance reminders on WhatsApp, and by SMS if WhatsApp does not reach you
- to answer your questions

## Who sees it

- our payment provider, to take your payment
- the courier or rider, so they can deliver to you
- our shipping and receiving partners, only as far as needed to deliver your goods

We do not sell your information.

## Your order number and privacy

To track an order you need both the order number and the phone number or email used to place it. This stops other people seeing your order.

## How long we keep it

> **To confirm with the owner.**

We keep order records for as long as needed to deliver, handle refunds, and meet the law. The exact period will be confirmed here.

## Your rights

You can ask us what we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it where the law allows.

## Data protection registration

> **To confirm with the owner.** The business needs to register with Ghana's Data Protection Commission because it stores customer names, phone numbers and addresses.
