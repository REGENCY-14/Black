## Cancelling before your goods ship

You can cancel any time **before your goods ship from China** and get a **full refund** to the payment method you used. Open your order on the [tracking page](/track) and choose **Request cancellation**. The button is only shown while cancelling is still possible.

## After your goods have shipped

Once your goods have shipped from China, the order can no longer be cancelled. Your goods are already on the way to Ghana.

## If a container is cancelled

If we cancel a container, we refund the deposit or payment for every order in it to the original payment method.

## Wrong, damaged or missing items

> **To confirm with the owner.** The store has not decided this yet.

If an item arrives wrong or damaged, or a factory cannot supply it, the owner will choose one of: a refund, a replacement, or store credit. This page will say which once it is decided. Until then, message us on WhatsApp straight away with a photo and your order number.

## Payment fees

We pay the payment provider's processing fee. It is never added to your total, and it is not taken out of a refund.

## How long a refund takes

> **To confirm with the owner.**

Refunds go back to the original payment method. The time it takes will be confirmed here. You can see the refund status on the tracking page.
