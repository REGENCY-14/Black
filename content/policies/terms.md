## Who we are

{{brand.name}} is a store that buys products from factories in China and ships them to customers in Ghana. You order before the goods arrive in Ghana. That is called a preorder.

## How a preorder works

1. You choose a product and pay a deposit, or pay in full.
2. We buy your items and ship them from China in a container.
3. You pay the balance before the container arrives, if you paid a deposit.
4. The container arrives in Ghana and we prepare your order.
5. Delivery is arranged directly between you and the rider.

You can follow every step on the tracking page with your order number.

## Prices

The price you see on the website includes shipping from China and import duty. It does not include delivery inside Ghana. Delivery is arranged directly between you and the courier or rider, and the fee is agreed with them.

All prices are in Ghana cedis (GH₵).

## Deposit and balance

- You can pay a deposit of **{{deposit.percent}}%** of the order total to confirm your preorder, or you can pay the full amount.
- If you pay a deposit, the balance is due **{{balance.dueDays}} days before the container arrives in Ghana**. The due date is shown at checkout, on your confirmation and on the tracking page.
- If that date has already passed when you order, we ask for full payment.
- **Your price is locked once your deposit is paid.** We do not add charges if exchange rates change.

## Items that arrive at different times

If your cart has items that arrive in different containers, we place them as separate orders. Each order has its own order number, deposit, balance, due date and delivery.

## Delivery times

Arrival windows are estimates. If something is delayed, the order shows as **Delayed** with a short note, and you keep your place. We tell you on WhatsApp, with SMS as a backup.

## If you do not pay the balance

> **To confirm with the owner.** This is the working rule until it is approved.

If the balance is not paid within {{balance.unpaidCancelDays}} days after the due date, the order is cancelled, the deposit is kept, and the item may be sold to someone else.
